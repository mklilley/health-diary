Personal voice diary

Health Diary

A self-hosted Telegram bot that turns voice notes into a personal health diary.

How it works

Send a voice note in a private chat with your bot. OpenAI transcribes the recording and creates a short summary. The bot replies in Telegram and saves the recording, transcript and summary in Google Drive, with a Google Sheets index.

Each installation supports one diary user and a separate administrator. The administrator can view diary summaries and manage the bot. Scheduled jobs create daily summaries and send reminders.

Google account access

The archive owner signs in with Google to authorise creation and maintenance of the diary folder and index. Access uses Google's per-file permission, covering files created or authorised through the app. The archive is created privately.

Read the privacy policy for details of the data processed, who can access it and how to request deletion.

For help with your diary, contact the person who provided your bot.
